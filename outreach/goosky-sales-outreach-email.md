## Why this email is built the way it is

- **It leads with their problem, not our history.** "UK demand is leaking to grey imports" is a reason for *them* to reply; "we are a great website" is not.
- **The ask is a numbered list.** A sales rep can answer it in five lines without writing prose, which dramatically raises reply rates.
- **It carries a commitment.** Naming an opening order — even a modest one — separates you from the enquiry-only traffic every manufacturer inbox gets.
- **It removes friction.** NDA, company details, call times, same-day turnaround: no reason to defer the reply.
- **One link, repeated twice.** The partner-tracked URL appears in the opening and in the signature, so the click is attributed however they read it.

## Follow-up plan

- **Day 4:** short reply-to-self bump — "just making sure this reached the right desk", re-attach nothing, keep it under 60 words.
- **Day 10:** second follow-up with one new piece of value (a mock-up of the Goosky brand page on the site, or a screenshot of search demand for "Goosky" in the UK).
- **Day 20:** final note offering to close the file, plus a direct line. Breakup emails have unusually high reply rates.
- Also try **WeChat / WhatsApp** or the contact form on gooskyrc.com if email goes quiet — hardware manufacturers in this segment often respond faster on messaging apps than email.
